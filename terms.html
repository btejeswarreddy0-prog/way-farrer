Using this prototype

Use the site to explore its account, vehicle-listing, request, and messaging features. Do not rely on a listing, price, request status, or chat as a verified offer or completed rental agreement.

Keep your sign-in details private. Listers are responsible for the accuracy and permission to publish information and photos they submit. Wayfarer does not currently verify vehicle ownership, driver eligibility, insurance, or listing claims.

Requests, messages, and payments

A request becomes an accepted trip only when the lister accepts it in the app. Messages support coordination for accepted requests. The prototype does not process or protect payments and does not currently provide dispute resolution or rental agreements. Do not arrange a real rental based only on this demo.

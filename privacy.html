Privacy notice

Demo notice: Wayfarer is currently a development project, not a launched rental service. This page summarizes how this prototype handles information; it is not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy before public launch.

Updated 29 October 2026.

Information used by the app

When you create an account, Wayfarer stores your email through Supabase Authentication and stores the name, mobile number, and renter or lister role in the Wayfarer profile. Listers can add vehicle details, prices, descriptions, locations, and photos. Renters can submit requested rental dates, pickup locations, destinations, and optional messages.

Who can see rental information

Published vehicle listings and their photos are made available to visitors so renters can browse them. Rental requests are available to the renter who sent them and the lister receiving them. Messages are available to those two participants when a request has been accepted. Access is enforced by Supabase row-level security policies.

Service providers and storage

This prototype uses Supabase for authentication, database records, and vehicle-image storage. Its project configuration and provider settings determine how and where data is processed and retained. Review those settings before using real personal information.

Payments and sensitive information

The prototype does not currently process payments. Do not enter payment-card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information into Wayfarer.
